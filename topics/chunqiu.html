春秋历史地图（前770—前476）· 疆域演变、州郡政区与古今地名

Spring and Autumn Historical Atlas (770 BCE – 476 BCE) · Capital: Luoyi (Zhou) · Jiang (Jin) · Ying (Chu) · Linzi (Qi)

起止年代：公元前770年 — 公元前476年 历代都城：洛邑（周）· 绛（晋）· 郢（楚）· 临淄（齐） 版图演变阶段：6 个时期快照 收录名城：45 座古城 编年大事：45 项关键节点

公元前770年周平王东迁洛邑，秦襄公因护送有功始列为诸侯，王畿缩小、共主衰微，诸侯渐强，史称春秋。齐桓公以“尊王攘夷”首霸中原，晋文公城濮败楚而接掌霸权，秦穆公西并戎狄，楚庄王问鼎洛邑，晋楚争霸贯穿两百年；后期向戌弭兵、吴越崛起，前506年吴师入郢，前473年越灭吴（本段止于前476年）。孔子、老子、管仲、子产、孙武等皆生于此期，封建秩序与礼乐传统在此定型又崩解（拖动滑块可看六阶段疆域与会盟、战事、城邑分布）。

In 770 BCE King Ping moved the Zhou capital east to Luoyi and Lord Xiang of Qin was first enfeoffed as a feudal lord; royal authority shrank and the feudal states rose — the Spring and Autumn period. Duke Huan of Qi led the states under ‘revere the king, expel the barbarians’; Duke Wen of Jin defeated Chu at Chengpu and took over the hegemony; Duke Mu of Qin absorbed the Rong in the west; King Zhuang of Chu asked the weight of the Zhou tripods. Jin–Chu rivalry ran for two centuries, followed by the Xiang Xu peace conference and the rise of Wu and Yue: Wu sacked Ying in 506 BCE and Yue destroyed Wu in 473 BCE (this era ends in 476 BCE). Confucius, Laozi, Guan Zhong, Zichan and Sun Wu all lived in this period (drag across six stages).
